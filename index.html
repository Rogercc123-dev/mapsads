How MapsAds works
Advertising inventory on a live world map.
1

Pick a country

Every country is an advertising placement. Free countries can be bought at their current asking price.

2

Publish your brand

Add your advertiser name, website, logo file and message. Your details appear on your territory.

3

Grow your footprint

Buy neighbouring countries to expand. Your brand remains one unified marker and grows with your total territory.

4

Get outbid

Claimed territory stays purchasable. A new advertiser can replace the current one by submitting a higher bid.
